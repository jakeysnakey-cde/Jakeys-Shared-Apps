About the prices and the plan
  • Prices came from ShopSaver on 7 November 2026. Some Coles and Woolworths prices are older because Coles' site was blocking price checks; the price list notes say which.
  • The list buys whole packs. Part-used packs are shared across the fortnight's recipes before rounding up, and swaps prefer meals that keep you under budget.
  • Leftover lunches use last night's dinner, so the dinner gets a little extra.
  • It's one shop for the fortnight. Freeze half the meat and one loaf of bread when you get home, and move them to the fridge the night before.
  • Chilli con carne and dumplings with broccolini are on every week, marked with a pin. ALDI doesn't sell broccolini, so the list has a head of broccoli there instead.
  • For Dexter: nothing has added salt, honey or zucchini. Sausages are cut lengthways, and bananas are kept for snacks.
